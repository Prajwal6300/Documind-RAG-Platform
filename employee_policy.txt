Employee Leave Policy

This document outlines the leave policy for all employees.

1. Casual Leave: Every employee is entitled to 12 days of casual leave per year.
2. Sick Leave: Every employee is entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year.
3. Annual Leave: After one year of employment, employees earn 20 days of annual leave.

Working hours are from 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday through Friday.

To request leave, employees must submit a request at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal.
